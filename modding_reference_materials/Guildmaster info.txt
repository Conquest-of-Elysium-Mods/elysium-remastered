Merchants Guild
The Guildmaster upgrades a Traders Guild into a Merchants Guild. Income and trade is increased by an additional +1 and the worldwide trade/gold bonus is increased to +20%. Multiple traders guild will increase the worldwide bonus further.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Apothecaries Guild
The Guildmaster establishes an Apothecaries Guild in a settlement with trade value of 1 or more. Occasionally Apothecary commanders can be recruited, this chance increases for every Apothecaries Guild established. Income is increased by +1. An Apothecary Apprentice joins the Guildmaster. Can only be cast in trade locations.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Mercenaries Guild
The Guildmaster establishes a Mercenaries Guild in a settlement with trade value of 1 or more. Will recruit special Guild Mercenaries automatically as well as increasing the number of ordinary mercenary offers, more mercenary guilds will make them even more frequent. The Mercenaries Guild reduces the income of the settlement by two gold per month.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Sages Guild
The Guildmaster establishes a Sages Guild in a settlement with trade value of 1 or more. The Guildmaster can occasionally recruit sages. Controlling at least one Sages Guild will result in all libraries yielding 1 gold income per level of the library. Building this guild will instantly reveal all libraries in Elysium. A Sage Apprentice joins the Guildmaster. Can only be cast in trade locations.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Miners Guild
The Guildmaster establishes a Miners Guild in a settlement with trade value of 1 or more. Gold income is increased by +1 and all income from mines is increased by 20% (gold, iron and gems). Multiple Miners Guilds will increase the mining bonus further.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Shipwrights Guild
The Guildmaster establishes a Shipwrights Guild in a Port. Shipwrights Guild allows the recruitment of Galleons and Mariners. If both a Shipwrights Guild and a Sages Guild have been established Mage Pilots will occasionally be available. If combined with a thieves guild in a pirate port, the pirates will get improved ships. Gold and trade is increased by +1 and all income from sea squares is increased by 25%.
Can only be cast in ports
Exit

Thieves Guild
The Guildmaster establishes a Thieves Guild in a settlement with trade value of 1 or more. The Thieves Guild increases income by +1. If captured by an enemy faction the settlement has its income reduced by 2. The thieves guild will produce brigand units in the settlement as well as in all brigand lairs. Multiple thieves guilds will make controlled brigand lairs spawn more brigands. A pirate port with a Thieves Guild occasionally generates a pirate ship that will independently raid and plunder the coastline.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Traders Guild
The Guildmaster establishes a Traders Guild in a settlement with trade value of 1 or more. Income is increased by +2 and trade is increased by +1. In addition there will also be a +10% worldwide bonus to gold income and trade. Multiple traders guild will increase the worldwide bonus further.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Assassination Contract
One assassin from every assassin guild (even in hostile settlements) will be hired to kill an important character of the chosen enemy faction. The contract can be signed at most once per half a year.
Can be cast anywhere
Exit

Assassins Guild
The Guildmaster upgrades a Thieves Guild into an Assassins Guild. The Guildmaster can occasionally recruit assassins at a reduced price. An assassin will appear in the guild once an assassination contract is signed. The Assassins Guild retains all features of the Thieves Guild and also increases the number of brigands and pirates produced.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit


Alchemists Guild
The Guildmaster upgrades an Apothecaries Guild into an Alchemists Guild. The Guildmaster can occasionally recruit Alchemists. The Alchemists Guild retains all features of the Apothecaries Guild. A Guild Alchemist Apprentice joins the Guildmaster. Can only be cast in trade locations.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Adventurers Guild
The Guildmaster upgrades a Mercenaries Guild into an Adventurers Guild. Adventurers will gather here and go out on adventures after a while. The Guildmaster can recruit heroes more often and for a lower price. The chance of receiving magic item offers is also increased. Multiple Adventurers Guilds stack their effects. The Adventurers Guild retains all features of the Mercenaries Guild.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Mages Guild
The Guildmaster upgrades a Sages Guild into a Mages Guild. The Library value of the settlement is increased by +1 and occasionally powerful wizards may be recruited. The Mages Guild retains all features of the Sages Guild. A Sage Adept bearing an Adept’s Spell Scroll joins the Guildmaster.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Masons Guild
The Guildmaster upgrades a Miners Guild into a Masons Guild. The masons guild will upgrade the fortification in the current location, maybe improving the gate, or maybe even creating a wall if there was none before. Mining bonus is increased to +40%. Local gold income is increased by an additional +1. The Masons Guild retains all features of the Miners Guild.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Cartographers Guild
The Guildmaster upgrades a Shipwrights Guild. A port with a Cartographers Guild can recruit Chartmakers occasionally. Gold and trade is increased by an additional +1 and the income increase for sea squares becomes 50%. The Cartographers Guild further improves any pirates and retains all features of the Shipwrights Guild.
Can only be cast in ports
Exit

Shadow Stalkers Guild
The Merchant Prince upgrades an Assassins Guild into a Shadow Stalkers Guild. The Shadow Stalkers Guild retains all features of the Assassins Guild and also increases the quality of the assassins and the number of brigands produced.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Bankers Guild
The Merchant Prince upgrades a Merchants Guild into a Bankers Guild. Income and trade is increased by an additional +1 and the worldwide trade/gold bonus is increased to +30%. Multiple traders guild will increase the worldwide bonus further.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit


Alchemist Magus Guild
The Merchant Prince upgrades an Alchemists Guild into an Alchemist Magus Guild. The Guildmaster can occasionally recruit Alchemist Magi. The Alchemist Magus Guild retains all features of the Alchemists Guild. A Guild Alchemist joins the Guildmaster.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Beastmasters Guild
The Merchant Prince upgrades an Adventurers Guild into a Beastmasters Guild. The beastmaster guild enables the recruitment of Pegasus Riders in Sky Cities and occasionally Gryphon Riders in the citadels of Elysium. Multiple Beastmasters Guilds increase the frequency of these offers. The Beastmasters Guild retains all features of the Adventurers Guild.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Arcane Guild
The Merchant Prince upgrades a Mages Guild into an Arcane Guild. The chance of recruiting powerful mages is increased. The Arcane Guild retains all features of the Mages Guild. An Initiate of the Metal Orders joins the Guildmaster.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Engineers Guild
The Merchant Prince upgrades a Masons Guild into an Engineers Guild. Income from mines is increased to +60%. Local gold income is increased by an additional +1 and fort defenses might be upgraded further. This guild enables the occasional recruitment of Mage Engineers if a Mage Guild is also owned by the Merchant Prince. More Engineers Guilds will increase this chance. The Masons Guild retains all features of the Miners Guild.
Can only be cast in trade locations
Exit

Void Pilots Guild
Gold and trade is increased by an additional +1 and the income increase for sea squares becomes 75%. The Void Pilots Guild enables the construction of Void Ships that can traverse the edge of the world and explore the Void.
Can only be cast in ports
Exit
